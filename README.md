# Senotherapeutics_Project_INGER-UACM
Cellular senescence, characterized by irreversible cell cycle arrest and secretion of proinflammatory factors through the senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP), is linked to multiple degenerative diseases such as type 2 diabetes, atherosclerosis, cancer, and neurodegenerative disorders.
In this study, advanced computational approaches were employed to identify senotherapeutic compounds:

Network pharmacology:
Construction of senescence-related protein-protein and drug-protein interaction networks.
Analysis of 65,339 compounds and their interactions with key process proteins.

Machine learning:
Models such as Random Forest, SVM and KNN ranked compounds according to their therapeutic potential.
Identification of 714 compounds with senotherapeutic activity, of which 270 showed optimal pharmacokinetic properties.

Outstanding results:
49 compounds already FDA-approved for other conditions, suggesting potential applications in anti-aging therapies.
Development of an interactive web tool (link) to consult the results, facilitating their use in translational research.
